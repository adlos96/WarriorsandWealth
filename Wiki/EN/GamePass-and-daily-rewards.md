# GamePass and daily rewards

*Silver and Gold: what they unlock, how rewards are claimed and why the streak must not be broken.*

---

## Two passes, two ways of being rewarded

A **GamePass** is a timed package bought in the [Shop](Shop-and-Diamonds.md) that unlocks extra rewards. There are two, designed for different players: the <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Silver_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **GamePass Silver** rewards those who play steadily during the month, the <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Gold_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **GamePass Gold** rewards those who show up every day. Neither makes the village stronger on its own: they give no stat bonuses, but Diamonds and an extra Fief.

|  | <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Silver_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt="">GamePass Silver | <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Gold_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt="">GamePass Gold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it unlocks | The second row of rewards of the [Monthly Quests](Monthly-Quests.md) | The daily rewards, one day after the other |
| What you have to do | Play and complete Quests | Log in every day |
| Where you see it | **Monthly Quests** tab, under the bar | **GamePass** tab |
| Duration | Timed, shown on the Shop card; you read the time left in the **Statistics** tab |  |

Time is bought in packs and accumulates: buying another one before expiry extends the duration, up to a maximum that the Shop card reminds you of (and that turns the button off when you reach it).

## <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Silver_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt="">GamePass Silver

With Silver active, every milestone of the Quest bar gives you *two* rewards instead of one: the **Normal** one, which belongs to everyone, and the <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Silver_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **GamePass Silver** one, drawn under the bar. At the end of the bar there is also a **Legendary Fief**. How they're claimed and what happens at the end of the month is told on the [Monthly Quests](Monthly-Quests.md) page.

> [!TIP]
> **When it's worth it**
>
> Silver pays off the most if you activate it *early* in the month and play regularly, because its rewards unlock with the same points as the Normal ones. If you activate it mid-month, the ones already passed can be claimed right away, but the time that has gone by in the meantime doesn't come back.

## <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Gold_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt="">GamePass Gold: the daily rewards

Gold is a long series of **ninety consecutive days**, with a reward for each day. In the **GamePass** tab you find two panels: at the top the pass status, with the time left and the «Day X of 90» indication; below, the **Daily Rewards**, a grid with all ninety days, each with its value in <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteViola_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Purple Diamonds**.

### How to claim

1. **Log in to the game.** Logging in once during the day is enough: it's the login that moves the streak on to the next day.
2. **Open the GamePass tab.** The reward of the day is the one highlighted as ready; days already taken are marked, future ones are locked.
3. **Tap it.** The Diamonds arrive immediately, with a small animation and a confirmation message.

### The rules of the streak

- **One reward a day** — You can only claim the reward of the current day of the streak, not those of past days. If you don't claim it today, tomorrow you'll move on to the next day and today's can't be recovered.
- **Skipping a day is costly** — If a whole day goes by without you logging in, the streak restarts from **Day 1** and the rewards claimed are reset. The game writes it clearly in the tab: be careful.
- **The pass must be active** — Rewards can only be claimed with Gold active. If it expires, at the first following login the streak resets: renewing it in time protects the accumulated days.
- **Same value for everyone** — Rewards are always in Purple Diamonds and the table is the same for every player. You read each day's value on its tile.

> [!WARNING]
> **When the day changes**
>
> The change of day is decided by the server's clock, not by your phone's. If you play around midnight, remember that the new day starts for everyone at the same moment.

## Don't confuse it with VIP

The <img src="../../assets/mini/Vip_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **VIP** is something else: it isn't a GamePass and it's bought separately, in its own Shop category. It gives real bonuses to the strength and economy of the village, which add to those of Technology and items (see [Inventory and items](Inventory-and-items.md)), but it gives no Diamonds and unlocks no rewards. You find the precise value of the bonuses in the **ⓘ** box of its card. You can have it together with one or both passes: they are independent.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>Can I have Silver and Gold together?</b></summary>

Yes, they are two independent packages, each with its own duration.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>If I buy Gold, is the first day's reward available right away?</b></summary>

Yes: the streak starts from Day 1 and you can claim the reward as soon as the pass is active.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I forgot to claim yesterday's reward: can I take it today?</b></summary>

No. Each day is only valid on its own day: today you can claim today's.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What happens if the pass expires halfway through the streak?</b></summary>

Rewards stop and, at the first login after expiry, the streak resets. If you renew it before it expires, the streak continues.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is the GamePass bought with Diamonds?</b></summary>

No, it's paid with real money: on the website in USDT and in the Android app through Google Play. The details are on the [Shop and Diamonds](Shop-and-Diamonds.md) page.

</details>
