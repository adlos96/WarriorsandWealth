# Kingdoms

*The world is divided into small communities: find out what you share with your neighbors and how to move house.*

---

## A world divided into Kingdoms

Warriors & Wealth is not a single map where everyone plays together: it is divided into **Kingdoms**. Each Kingdom is a community of villages that share the same opponents, the same Barbarian Cities and the same Rallies. Think of it as a small server inside the server: in your Kingdom you play, fight and cooperate; outside your Kingdom nobody can reach you, and you can't reach anybody.

## How you enter a Kingdom

When you create a new village, the game assigns it **on its own** to the first Kingdom with a free place: you don't have to choose anything. Each Kingdom hosts a limited number of villages (you can see the capacity in the Kingdoms menu). When all the existing Kingdoms are full, a new one is born, with its Barbarian Cities already ready. That is why the oldest Kingdoms are the most crowded and the new ones, at the beginning, are almost empty. Sometimes a Kingdom can be closed by the administrators: it no longer welcomes new arrivals, but those who live there keep playing normally.

You can see at any time all the Kingdoms, how full they are and which one is yours from **player menu → Kingdoms**.

## What you share with your Kingdom

|  | In your Kingdom | Outside your Kingdom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PVP and Espionage | The villages of the other players in the Kingdom | Out of reach |
| Rallies | You can create them and join those of your Kingdom | You can't join the Rallies of other Kingdoms |
| Barbarian Cities | The same twenty Cities for everyone, with shared stocks | Each Kingdom has its own |
| Barbarian Villages | Personal: everyone has their own | Personal: everyone has their own |
| Your village | Resources, buildings, army and research are yours | They stay yours even if you change Kingdom |

### Barbarian Cities are alive

Each Kingdom has its **twenty Barbarian Cities**, one for each difficulty level, identical for all its inhabitants. They are truly shared, and this changes the way you play:

- **Wounds remain.** The troops a City loses during an attack don't come back on their own: whoever arrives after you finds it weaker, and whoever came before has weakened it in your place.
- **Stocks run out.** Everything that gets plundered leaves the City and is no longer there for the others, until it regenerates.
- **Every month starts over.** On the first day of the month all the Cities become new again, with defenses and stocks at the maximum.

The **Barbarian Villages**, instead, are personal: everyone has their own, they renew every month together with the Cities and follow your level. The [Barbarian Cities and Villages](Barbarian-Cities-and-Villages.md) page talks about them. They don't depend on anyone else and don't change if you change Kingdom.

## Cities tailored to the Kingdom

Barbarian Cities are not equally strong in every Kingdom: the game looks at how strong the most advanced active players are and adapts the Cities accordingly. A Kingdom of veterans will have harder but also richer Cities; a young Kingdom, with players still at a low level, will have Cities that are more within reach. The adaptation happens once a month, when the Cities regenerate.

## The Purple Diamonds of the Cities

Purple Diamonds are the rarest resource in the game and Barbarian Cities are one of the few sources to earn them by playing. That is why their flow is carefully regulated, so that many receive them and not only the strongest.

- **There is a monthly reserve.** All the Cities of the Kingdom, together, contain a limited amount: when it's gone, it's gone until the next month.
- **There is a weekly limit per Account.** Each Account can take only a certain amount of Purple from the Cities every week, and the counter restarts from zero every Monday. If you have several villages linked to the same Account, the limit is single and shared among them.
- **You see it while you attack.** When you attack a City, the game shows you how much Purple you can still obtain. Once the limit is reached, the Cities keep giving you all the other resources: only the Purple stops.
- **You lose nothing.** What you don't take stays in the Cities, for you and for the others, and a part of what is left over also passes to the next month.

> [!NOTE]
> **A limit that adapts**
>
> The limit is not a fixed number: the game adjusts it carefully, based on how many active players there are in the Kingdom and how the month is going. A newly opened Kingdom starts with a lower limit. To know how much you can still take, always refer to what the client shows you.

PVP also has its own limits on Diamonds, independent from those of the Cities: you find them in the dedicated guide.

## Changing Kingdom

You can move your village to another Kingdom, for example to join friends or to look for a less crowded Kingdom. You lose nothing: resources, buildings, army and research stay. What changes are the opponents, the Barbarian Cities and the Rallies, which are those of the new Kingdom.

You need the **Kingdom change** item, which is bought in the **Shop**, among the Items, in Purple Diamonds: you see the price there. The item is consumed on its own at the moment of the transfer.

1. Get a **Kingdom change** in the Shop.
2. Open **player menu → Kingdoms** (or press «Use» on the item from the Inventory).
3. Choose the destination Kingdom and press **Move here**, then confirm.

For the transfer to succeed, all of these conditions must be true:

| Condition | Why |
| --- | --- |
| You have a Kingdom change item | It's the «ticket» for the trip. |
| You don't have the Peace Shield active | It prevents using the change to escape an attack without risk. |
| You have no Rallies in progress | First close or leave the Rallies you take part in. |
| The waiting time since the last change has passed | It prevents continuous jumping from one Kingdom to another. You see the time left in the Kingdoms menu. |
| The destination Kingdom is neither full nor closed | There must be a free place for you. |

> [!WARNING]
> **Good to know**
>
> When you change Kingdom, Rallies launched **against you** close on their own, because you're no longer a reachable target (the troops of those who took part go back home). The weekly limit of the Cities' Purple Diamonds is tied to your Account and not to the Kingdom: moving doesn't earn you a second limit in the same week.

## When a Kingdom empties out: the Merge

Over time some players stop playing and a Kingdom can be left with few active inhabitants. In these cases the administrators can **merge** two Kingdoms, moving everyone to a livelier one. Active players are brought to the new Kingdom immediately and receive a **Peace Shield** to settle in without being attacked; inactive ones are moved later, at their next login. Whoever has a Rally in progress is moved only after closing it. In the end the empty Kingdom is deleted. The move is free and costs you nothing of your village.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>Can I play with my friends?</b></summary>

Yes, if you are in the same Kingdom: you can create [Rallies](Rallies.md) together and split the Cities. If you are in different Kingdoms, whoever wants to join the others can use a Kingdom change, as long as there is room.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why is there nobody to attack in my Kingdom?</b></summary>

PVP only concerns the players of your Kingdom and has a minimum access level. In a sparsely populated Kingdom there are few opponents: you can consider moving to a livelier one.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is a new Kingdom better than an old one?</b></summary>

It depends on what you're looking for. In a new Kingdom the Cities are more within reach and there's less competition, but there are few players. A Kingdom of veterans has harder and richer Cities, and more opponents.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How many Kingdoms are there?</b></summary>

As many as needed: they open on their own when the previous ones fill up. You always find the updated list in the Kingdoms menu.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>If I change Kingdom does the weekly Purple Diamonds limit restart?</b></summary>

No. The counter belongs to your Account and resets only on Monday: if during a week you've been in two Kingdoms, the sum of what you've taken counts.

</details>
