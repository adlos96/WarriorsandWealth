# Experience and levels

*How Experience is earned, how you level up and what it is for.*

---

## The mark of your growth

Two numbers tell how far you've come: the <img src="../../assets/mini/Livello_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Level** and the <img src="../../assets/mini/Exp_1.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Experience**. Experience accumulates as you play and, when it reaches the right amount, it raises your Level. There is no bar to watch anxiously: they're simple numbers that grow, quietly, every time you fight.

You find them in the top bar: the icon with the **Level** number and the one with the accumulated **Experience**, followed by *XP*. Tapping either one opens a description that, for Experience, also tells you how much you need to reach the next level.

## How Experience is earned

Experience comes from a single source: **battles**. Building, researching or training don't give any: to grow you have to fight. But you don't need to win to earn it, because what counts is what you do on the field.

The Report of every battle shows the Experience of the whole clash, phase by phase, and the total you earned at the bottom. [Battle reports](Battle-reports.md) talks about it.

> [!NOTE]
> **Don't confuse it with Quests**
>
> In the Monthly Quests you'll see points called «Exp». They are the points of the Quest bar, a separate count: they don't raise your <img src="../../assets/mini/Livello_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Level**. How they work will be explained by the dedicated page.

## Leveling up

When Experience reaches the required amount, the level goes up and the surplus isn't lost: it's carried over to the next level. If you have a lot of Experience, you can go up several levels at once. Each level costs more than the previous one, so the first ones come quickly and then the pace slows down. You read the amount required for the next level by tapping the Experience icon.

At the moment there is no maximum level: you can keep growing as long as you feel like fighting.

## What the Level is for

- **Technology points** — Every level you gain gives you one **technology point**, to spend in the Technology tree. It's the most concrete reward: the higher you go, the stronger and more specialized your village can become. See [Research and Technology](Research-and-Technology.md).
- **Advanced research** — Some research requires a minimum player <img src="../../assets/mini/Livello_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Level**, which grows with the level of the research itself. The Research screen tells you what you're missing when you can't start one yet.
- **Challenges tailored to you** — Barbarian Villages are generated on your level at the moment of renewal, and PVP only opens for those who have reached the unlock level, which you find shown in the **PVP** panel.
- **Level Quests** — Some Monthly Quests reward leveling up itself, one more reason to fight regularly.

## Level is not strength

A high level says that you've fought a lot, not that your village is strong. A village's strength depends on structures, army and research, and it's what you see in the power indicator of the PVP list. A mid-level player with a well-equipped army and well-kept defenses can be more fearsome than a more advanced but neglectful one: don't trust the level number alone.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> **Growing with method**
>
> **Fight regularly.** Barbarian Villages are the safest and most consistent way to accumulate Experience, especially at the beginning. **Plan your technology points.** A point spent stays forever, so it's worth choosing carefully where to put it; but one left waiting doesn't strengthen the village. **Defend well.** Even repelling an attack gives you Experience: a solid defense makes you grow and protects you at the same time.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>Do I lose Experience if I lose a battle?</b></summary>

No. Nothing in the game takes away the Experience you've earned: a defeat costs you the fallen troops, not your level.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does building a structure or finishing a research give Experience?</b></summary>

No. Experience only comes from battles.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>If I go up several levels at once, do I receive all the technology points?</b></summary>

Yes: every level gained is worth a point, even when you pass more than one with the same battle.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I level up even while I'm offline?</b></summary>

Yes, if you're attacked and repel the assault: the defender's Experience arrives anyway and, when you return, you find your level updated.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What is the maximum level?</b></summary>

At the moment there is no limit.

</details>
