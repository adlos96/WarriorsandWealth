# Inventory and items

*Consumable items: where to buy them, the six categories, the rules of timed bonuses and the special items.*

---

## The toolbox

**Items** are consumables that give you an advantage when you need it: a resource refill, a few extra soldiers, a production bonus, a bit of time saved. They do nothing while they sit in storage: you use them, at the moment you choose, from the **Inventory** tab in the bottom bar.

They are bought in the **Shop**, by pressing **Items** at the top (next to **Packs**), paying with **Diamonds**: Blue or Purple, depending on the item. What is worth buying and how much it costs is seen there; this page explains what they do once you have them.

## How the tab works

Each type of item has its own card, with the icon (and in a corner the total you own), the name and a short description. Almost always the same item exists in several *sizes*: for example a shorter or longer bonus, a smaller or bigger pack. The sizes are the buttons under the description and show the value and, next to it, how many you have. To use one:

1. Choose the **category** with the buttons at the top.
2. Tap the **size** you want to use.
3. If the item can be used in series (packs, speed-ups, troops, traps, shields), set how many with the **−**, **+** and **Max** buttons.
4. Press **Use**. Timed bonuses ask for a confirmation, because you won't be able to activate another identical one until it expires.

By default you only see the items you own. By ticking **Also show items you do not own** the whole catalog appears: useful to know what exists before buying it. Some items are marked *Coming soon*: you can see them, but they can't be used yet.

## The six categories

- **Boosts** — Timed bonuses. They raise the **Health**, the **Defense** or the **Attack** of the whole army in battle; civilian or military production; they reduce the times of *new* constructions, trainings and researches. Also here are the **Scout**, which strengthens your spies, and the **Counter-scout**, which makes the village immune to espionage by other players (see [Espionage](Espionage.md)).
- **Resources** — The *packs*: they immediately add the indicated amount of a resource to the storage. There are civilian ones, military ones, <img src="../../assets/mini/Frecce_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Arrows**, <img src="../../assets/mini/Popolazione_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Population** and also **Diamonds**. They are used to cover a production gap without waiting.
- **Troops** — They directly add soldiers to the Barracks, by class and by level. They only work if the level is already unlocked with **Technology** and if there are enough free places: items that don't fit are not consumed.
- **Traps** — Like troops, but for **Traps**: you need the trap unlocked and free places in the Workshops. They are added to the village and then you deploy them yourself, as explained in [The City and defense](The-City-and-defense.md).
- **Time** — The *speed-ups* shorten the constructions, trainings or researches already in progress and in the queue, and use only the items that are really needed. **Builders**, **Recruiters** and **Trap makers** instead open an extra queue for a while, like the same-named Shop packs, up to a maximum.
- **Special** — The items outside the other categories: the **Peace Shield**, the **Technology reset** and the **Kingdom change**. Other items still *Coming soon* also appear here.

## The rules of timed bonuses

Boosts are the most delicate to use, because they have precise rules:

- **One per type.** For each type of bonus only one can be active at a time, of any size. Until it expires the **Use** button stays off and the card tells you how much time is left.
- **Time runs offline too.** You don't need to stay connected: the bonus keeps being consumed.
- **They add to the rest.** An item's bonus adds to those of **Technology** and <img src="../../assets/mini/Vip_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **VIP**; it doesn't replace them.
- **Active bonuses are visible.** In the Inventory tab you find them in the **Active bonuses** box, with the countdown, and also in the **Active items** section of Statistics.

> [!TIP]
> **The right moment**
>
> The construction, training and research time bonuses only count for the activities you *start* while they are active. It's better to prepare them beforehand and launch them all after activating the bonuses, so the advantage isn't wasted. For a battle bonus the same applies: activate it before attacking or being attacked, not after.

## Three items to know well

### Peace Shield

It protects the village from player attacks. Unlike normal bonuses, the shield's time *adds* to the one already active, up to a maximum limit; if the addition would exceed it, the game refuses the use and spends nothing. Remember that while the shield is active you can't attack other players (see [PVP: challenging players](PVP-challenging-players.md)). You also can't activate it while there is a [Rally](Rallies.md) open against you.

### Technology reset

It clears all the points spent in the Technology tree, which become available to reassign. It's a heavy action, and for this reason it always asks for confirmation: the bonuses and the units or traps unlocked with Technology are lost until you reassign them (see [Research and Technology](Research-and-Technology.md)). If you have no points assigned the game doesn't consume it.

### Kingdom change

It is used to move the village to another Kingdom. By pressing **Use** from the card you don't consume it: the Kingdoms menu opens, and the item is spent only when you actually choose where to go (see [Kingdoms](Kingdoms.md)).

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Why is <b>Use</b> off?</summary>

There are two reasons. If it's a timed bonus, you already have one of the same type active: the card shows the time left. If it's a troop or trap item, the level it brings hasn't been unlocked with Technology yet: the card says so.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I used troop packs but fewer entered than expected.</b></summary>

Because the Barracks (or the Workshops, for traps) didn't have enough free places. Only the items that fit went in; the others stayed in the Inventory.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use several bonuses of the same type one after the other?</b></summary>

Yes, but one at a time: as soon as the first expires you can activate the next one.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>If I leave the game does the bonus stop?</b></summary>

No. Time runs even while you're offline.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do I buy items?</b></summary>

In the Shop, by pressing Items. They are paid with Diamonds, Blue or Purple depending on the item.

</details>
