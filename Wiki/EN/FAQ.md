# Frequently asked questions

*Short answers to the most common questions about the village, battles, Diamonds and account.*

---

## The questions that come up most often

Here you find short answers to the most common questions, grouped by topic. Each answer points to the page that goes into detail, and each page has its own more specific frequently asked questions at the bottom. If you don't find what you're looking for, try the search at the top or write to **Support** from the player menu.

## Getting started

<details>
<summary><b>Where should I start?</b></summary>

From the [Quick start](Quick-start.md): it tells you in what order to move in the first hours. If instead you want an overview of the game, there's [Welcome to Warriors & Wealth](Introduction.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need to stay logged in for the village to grow?</b></summary>

No. Production, construction, training and research continue even while you're offline, and when you come back you find the result. The opposite is also true, though: the village can be attacked while you're away, so before leaving check the defenses and, if you want a break from the wars, use the **Peace Shield**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can you play without spending?</b></summary>

Yes. **Diamonds** are also earned by playing, and the Shop is a way to support the game and save time, not an obligation. How Diamonds are obtained is in the dedicated section below.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I play both on the computer and on the phone?</b></summary>

Yes: the game works on PC, tablet and phone, and there's also the Android app. You log in with the same Account and find the same village.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Who can play?</b></summary>

The game is designed for those who are at least 14 years old. The Shop on the website and withdrawals are reserved for adults, who confirm it with a declaration.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do I find the exact numbers for costs, times and bonuses?</b></summary>

In the game itself. Press the **i** or **ⓘ** button of a row, a Shop card or a panel, or tap a resource's icon: the real, up-to-date values appear. The wiki explains the rules and tells you where to look, because numbers may be adjusted over time.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I don't know a word used in the game: where do I look it up?</b></summary>

In the [Glossary](Glossary.md), which explains the game's terms in a few lines and links to the full pages.

</details>

## Village and economy

<details>
<summary><b>Why can't I build or train?</b></summary>

Almost always something is missing: resources, Population, a free place in the Barracks or a level still locked by Technology. The game writes which one. The full conditions are in [Construction and buildings](Construction-and-buildings.md) and [Army](Army.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>A resource stopped growing: why?</b></summary>

It has probably reached its storage **limit**: spend it and production restarts. If instead the stocks are dropping, the net production is negative, meaning troops and structures consume more than you produce. [Resources](Resources.md) talks about it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does the second structure cost more than the first?</b></summary>

Because every structure of the same type costs a bit more than the previous one. It's best to expand the village in steps; you can always see the exact price of the next one in the **i** box of the row.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I spent my Technology points badly: can I fix it?</b></summary>

Only with the **Technology reset** item, which clears the tree and returns all the points to reassign. See [Research and Technology](Research-and-Technology.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>An item can't be used: what do I check?</b></summary>

If it's a timed bonus, you already have one of the same type active; if it's a troop or trap pack, the level it brings may not be unlocked yet. The item's card says so. See [Inventory and items](Inventory-and-items.md).

</details>

## Battles and Kingdoms

<details>
<summary><b>How do I protect myself from attacks?</b></summary>

With four things: assign troops to the City's structures, deploy the traps, keep the defenses repaired and, when you want a break, activate the Peace Shield. The full picture is in [The City and defense](The-City-and-defense.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Whom can I attack?</b></summary>

The Barbarians at any moment. Other players only if they're in your Kingdom, if they've reached the PVP unlock level and if neither of you has the Peace Shield active. See [PVE: Barbarians](PVE-Barbarians.md) and [PVP: challenging players](PVP-challenging-players.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I lost a battle: how do I understand what went wrong?</b></summary>

Open the **Report**: the last phase of the report is where your army stopped, and a «Low on arrows» warning explains many unexpected defeats. [Battle reports](Battle-reports.md) teaches you how to read it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it worth exploring before attacking?</b></summary>

Almost always yes: it costs a bit of Gold, but it tells you what the target defends and avoids sending the army to ruin. See [Espionage](Espionage.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I play with my friends?</b></summary>

Yes, if you're in the same **Kingdom**: you can create Rallies together and split the Barbarian Cities. If you're in different Kingdoms, one of you can use a Kingdom change, as long as there's room in the chosen Kingdom. See [Kingdoms](Kingdoms.md) and [Rallies](Rallies.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I lose my village if I change Kingdom?</b></summary>

No. Resources, buildings, army and research stay yours: only the opponents, Barbarian Cities and Rallies change, which are those of the new Kingdom.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What happens on the first of the month?</b></summary>

The barbarian world renews itself, and the Monthly Quests start over from zero with their rewards. What you haven't claimed before the end of the month is lost: see [Barbarian Cities and Villages](Barbarian-Cities-and-Villages.md) and [Monthly Quests](Monthly-Quests.md).

</details>

## Diamonds, packs and rewards

<details>
<summary><b>How are Diamonds earned by playing?</b></summary>

<img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteBlu_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Blue Diamonds** come from Quests, Barbarian loot and the exchange of Purple. <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteViola_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Purple Diamonds** from Quests, GamePass rewards and Barbarian Cities, with weekly limits designed to distribute them fairly; when available, the Overview's **Free reward** box also gives some in exchange for a watched ad.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I exchange Blue Diamonds into Purple?</b></summary>

No, the exchange goes in one direction only: from Purple to Blue.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Are the Fiefs' Tributes guaranteed forever?</b></summary>

No. Warriors & Wealth tries to always keep their generation active and would like to do so for as long as possible, but reserves the right to change or remove it. They should therefore be experienced as a part of the game and not as an income to count on.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Are VIP and GamePass the same thing?</b></summary>

No. The <img src="../../assets/mini/Vip_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **VIP** gives bonuses to the village's strength and economy; the **GamePasses** give no bonuses but Diamonds and an extra Fief. You can have them together. See [GamePass and daily rewards](GamePass-and-daily-rewards.md) and [Shop and Diamonds](Shop-and-Diamonds.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does the pack I bought apply to all my villages?</b></summary>

No: purchases go to the village you're logged in with at the moment you pay.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I paid but the reward didn't arrive.</b></summary>

Wait for the payment to be confirmed: the request status tells you where it stands. If something doesn't add up, write to Support from the player menu, saying what you bought.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I missed the GamePass Gold daily reward: can I recover it?</b></summary>

No. Each day is only valid on its own day, and if a whole day goes by without logging in the streak restarts from Day 1. See [GamePass and daily rewards](GamePass-and-daily-rewards.md).

</details>

## Account and security

<details>
<summary><b>I forgot my password: what do I do?</b></summary>

On the login screen press **Forgot password?**: the game guides you with a code sent by email. If you can't manage, below you also find «Need help? Contact Support», which works even without having logged in.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I switch to another of my villages?</b></summary>

From the player menu, which opens from your name at the top, choose **Change Village**. In the same menu you also find the profile picture change, the Kingdoms, Support, Options and Privacy policy.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I make the account more secure?</b></summary>

In **Options** you can enable two-factor authentication (**2FA**) via email: at login and before every withdrawal you're asked for a code received by mail. Never share your password or codes with anyone, not even with someone claiming to be part of the team.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I contact the team?</b></summary>

With the **Support** entry in the player menu. You can send a complaint, report a bug or report another player; you find the replies in the same Support, where a dot flags news. If you write from the login screen, without having logged in, the reply arrives by email.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I want to delete my account: how do I do it?</b></summary>

From **Options**, **Account deletion** entry, press **Request account deletion** and confirm with your password. The account is suspended immediately and deleted after 7 days: during this period you can cancel by logging in again. Deletion is permanent, and the <img src="../../assets/mini/Tributi_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Tributes** you have at that moment are lost; only the records the law requires us to keep remain.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do I read how my data is handled?</b></summary>

In the **Privacy policy**, which you find in the player menu.

</details>
