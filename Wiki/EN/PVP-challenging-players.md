# PVP: challenging players

*Challenging other players in the Kingdom: who you can attack, how the clash unfolds, loot and limits.*

---

## Challenging another ruler

**PVP** is the clash between players: you choose an opponent, send your army and, if you manage to get through their defenses, you come home with the loot. It's the riskiest part of the game, but also the only one where the enemy thinks as much as you: they built their defenses, assigned the garrisons, deployed the traps. Whoever wins well is whoever prepared the most.

In the **PVP/PVE** tab press **PVP**. Until you reach the required level the panel is locked and tells you so (you read the exact level there). The troops to send, as always, are chosen only once in the **Army to Send** panel.

## Who you can attack

You don't choose from an endless list: the game offers you a list of opponents suited to you.

- **Only from your Kingdom.** The other Kingdoms are out of reach: the [Kingdoms](Kingdoms.md) page talks about it.
- **With a strength similar to yours.** The list favors opponents of comparable power; if there are few, the game gradually widens the comparison, so as to always offer you someone.
- **Never your other villages.** If you have several villages on the same Account, they don't attack each other.
- **Not those who are protected.** Whoever has the **Peace Shield** active doesn't appear in the list, and neither does whoever hasn't reached the PVP unlock level yet.

> [!WARNING]
> **The shield works in both directions**
>
> If you have the **Peace Shield** active, you can't attack other players: the game warns you and nothing starts. Against the Barbarians, instead, you can keep fighting. If you want to attack, wait for the shield to end.

## The list of opponents

Each opponent is a card with the <img src="../../assets/mini/Livello_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Level**, the name and a **power** indicator colored relative to yours: it tells you at a glance whether they are weaker, similar or stronger than you. These are rough indications: power adds up structures, army and research, but doesn't say how the defenses are deployed. That is why espionage exists.

On each card you also see how many **Purple and Blue Diamonds** you can still plunder from that opponent. The number is the lower of what the limit grants you and what the limit protects for them: it doesn't reveal the diamonds they own.

At the top you find the **Weekly diamond limits** box, with two rows: **Plunder** (how many diamonds you've taken this week with attacks and PVP Rallies) and **Lost** (how many you've lost as a defender). Next to it is the renewal countdown, which happens every Monday.

## How to attack

1. Choose the troops in **Army to Send**.
2. Tap the card of the opponent you're interested in.
3. If you want, press **Explore**: your spies cost <img src="../../assets/mini/Oro_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Gold** and bring you a Report with what they managed to find out (see [Espionage](Espionage.md)).
4. Press **Attack**. The clash is resolved immediately and the result arrives in the **Reports**.

## Inside the battle

The attack follows the order of your opponent's defenses, from the outside in. Each of the six layers (**Entrance, Walls, Gate, Towers, Center, Castle**) is a clash of its own, and at the end comes the **Village**, where the soldiers left at home fight. In each clash:

1. If the structure has **Traps**, they go into action against the assailants and weaken them.
2. The **ranged units** of both sides speak. <img src="../../assets/mini/Arciere_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Archers** and <img src="../../assets/mini/Catapulta_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Catapults** consume <img src="../../assets/mini/Frecce_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Arrows**, and without ammunition they fight far below their value.
3. Then the **melee**: here the structure's **Health** and **Defense** act as a cushion, absorbing blows along with the soldiers garrisoning it.
4. Whoever still has troops standing moves on to the next layer; fallen assailants don't come back.

Success is decided in the last clash, the one in the **Village**: if in the end the defenders left at home have been eliminated, the attack succeeded and you can plunder. But to get there with an army still standing you must have worn down all the previous layers well: an army that fizzles out on the way comes home without loot.

## The loot

If you win, your survivors plunder **a part** of the opponent's civilian resources, and never all of them: **Food, Wood, Stone, Iron and Gold**. How much you manage to carry away depends on the **carrying capacity** of the troops left standing, so it isn't just about winning but also how you win. <img src="../../assets/mini/Popolazione_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Population**, military resources and Tribute stocks can't be touched.

**Diamonds** follow stricter rules: you take them only if neither your weekly limit nor your opponent's is exhausted. The limit protects defenders from a barrage of consecutive attacks and gives you a realistic cap on what you can earn. When the diamonds are exhausted, the plunder continues with the other resources.

## Experience, statistics and quests

Both players earn <img src="../../assets/mini/Exp_1.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Experience** for the enemy units eliminated, and the attacker also gets some for the traps that went off. Battles count in the statistics and can advance Quests. Experience and Level are covered on the dedicated page later on.

## When you are the one attacked

There is nothing to do at the moment it happens: the clash is resolved in an instant, even while you're offline. You receive a copy of the Report: layer by layer, traps that went off, losses and resources lost. What you can do before and after:

- **Before:** assign the garrisons, deploy the traps, keep the defenses repaired ([The City and defense](The-City-and-defense.md)), don't leave the storages full, or activate the **Peace Shield** if you want a break from the wars (don't do it at the last minute: with a [Rally](Rallies.md) open against you it can no longer be bought or activated).
- **After:** read the Report, repair the damaged structures and rebuild the traps used. Lost troops need to be retrained.

If someone tried to spy on you before attacking, you may have found an *Espionage detected* notice: it's no guarantee of an incoming attack, but it's a good reason to check your defenses.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> **Thinking like a strategist**
>
> **Don't trust the color alone.** A «similar» opponent with well-distributed garrisons and deployed traps can be tougher than a «stronger» one left exposed: Explore shows you the difference. **Bring the Arrows.** Archers without ammunition are dead weight. **Weigh the risk.** Fallen soldiers don't come back, and a defeat costs you troops without giving you anything in return.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>Why is the list of opponents empty?</b></summary>

Your Kingdom may be sparsely populated, or the other players may have the Peace Shield active or not have unlocked PVP yet. The list updates on its own over time.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I attack someone from another Kingdom?</b></summary>

No. Only the players of your Kingdom are reachable, and for changing Kingdom you find the conditions on the Kingdoms page.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What happens if I lose?</b></summary>

You lose the fallen troops and bring home no loot. The survivors return to the village and the Report shows you in which layer the attack stopped.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is the opponent notified that I want to attack them?</b></summary>

No: the attack is resolved instantly and they find out from the Report. Espionage, on the other hand, can be discovered, as the dedicated page explains.

</details>
