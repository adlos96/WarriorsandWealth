# Espionage

*Explore: what it costs, what it reveals and how to defend yourself from other people's espionage.*

---

## Know before you strike

Sending the army against an unknown target is a gamble. **Espionage** exists to turn it into a decision: your spies observe an enemy (a Barbarian Village or City, but also another player) and bring you a report of what they saw. It isn't an attack, it does no damage and costs you no troops: it costs you <img src="../../assets/mini/Oro_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Gold**.

## How it's done

The button is called **Explore** and you find it next to **Attack**, both in the **Barbarians** panel of the **PVE** tab and in that of the **PVP** tab. Select the target, press **Explore** and after a moment you find the report in the **Reports** tab, titled *Espionage Report*.

- **It costs Gold.** The price depends on the level (yours or the target's, as the case may be); if you don't have enough, the game writes how much is needed and nothing starts.
- **The button rests.** After each press there is a short pause, to avoid double submissions.
- **For players, PVP rules apply.** You can only spy on players of your Kingdom, and only if both you and they have reached the PVP unlock level.

> [!WARNING]
> **For those who don't want to be spied on**
>
> The **Counter-scout** item makes the village immune to espionage for the indicated duration: whoever tries to spy on you gets a message and spends nothing. Conversely, the **Scout** item increases the strength of your spies. You find them among the inventory items.

## The strength of the spies

What your spies manage to find out depends on their **strength**. It grows with the **Espionage** research (and with Technology and item bonuses) and is reduced by the target's **Counter-Espionage**: the more prepared they are, the harder your spies struggle. The report shows the *Espionage strength* reached and the *Stage* that resulted from it.

Strength decides two distinct things:

| How much you see | How precise you are |
| --- | --- |
| The **Stage** determines which sections of the report open up. At minimum strength the mission *fails*; then, as it rises, you first see the resources, then the troops and defenses, then the buildings, the research and finally the bonuses and detailed statistics. | **Precision** decides whether a number is exact or an estimate. With inexperienced spies many values appear as a range (from… to…): the truth lies somewhere in between, but you don't know where. |

What the spies couldn't reach appears as **????**: the game shows it on purpose, to remind you that it exists and that with stronger spies you could find it out. At the bottom of the report, if needed, you also find an invitation to increase your espionage strength.

## What's in the report

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Header | Spied target, level, experience, espionage strength and Stage reached. With strong enough spies the target's Espionage and Counter-Espionage research levels also appear. |
| Storehouse and Military Stock | The civilian and military resources the target keeps: the possible loot. |
| Structures | One defensive layer at a time (Entrance, Walls, Gate, Towers, Center, Castle and Village): the troops garrisoning it, divided by level, the deployed **Traps** and, where they exist, Health, Defense and Garrison. |
| Civil Structures, Workshop, Barracks | How many structures they have built, that is, how developed the village is. «Workshop» are the Workshops. |
| Civil and Military Research | How far their general research and unit research have come. |
| Troops, Troop Bonuses, Village Bonuses | The complete picture of their units' statistics and the bonuses that strengthen them. |

## Reading a report

The most useful sections to decide on an attack are those of the **Structures**: with a strong enough spy you see, layer by layer, how many soldiers you'll have to face and how many traps await you. It's the information that separates a successful assault from a slaughter. For a Barbarian, instead, it's enough to look at the troops and weigh your strength against theirs.

> [!TIP]
> **The value of an estimate**
>
> A range isn't a lie: if the opponent's worst case is still below your strength, you can attack with confidence. If the best case puts you in trouble, reinforce or wait until you have more precise spies.

## If you're being spied on

Espionage isn't invisible. When someone tries to spy on you, there is a chance your sentries notice, and you receive a Report titled **Espionage detected**. The stronger the enemy spies, the harder they are to discover. Three possible cases:

- **Spy stopped.** If the attempt failed, your sentries always discover it and know the name of whoever tried.
- **Spy discovered but successful.** You know someone gathered information about you, but the name is only sometimes visible: the Report then speaks of an «unknown spy».
- **Spy not discovered.** You receive nothing and never know.

To make spying on you harder you need the **Counter-Espionage** research, the Technology bonuses or, for a period, the **Counter-scout** item. And remember: a discovered spy doesn't mean an incoming attack, but it's an excellent reason to check garrisons and repairs.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>Why did I spend Gold and the report says «Espionage failed»?</b></summary>

Because your spies weren't strong enough to overcome the target's Counter-Espionage. The Gold is spent anyway: before trying again, strengthen the Espionage research or use the Scout item.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do the Barbarians notice my spies?</b></summary>

No: they are targets without any warning. They only serve to know what you'll find.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The report gives me a range: should I trust it?</b></summary>

It's an honest estimate, but not an exact one. A more precise spy narrows the range until it shows the real value.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why do some sections show «????»?</b></summary>

Because the strength reached isn't yet enough to uncover that data. The game leaves them visible to indicate what can still be known.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do I find old reports?</b></summary>

In the **Reports** tab of the PVP/PVE screen: you can reopen them, mark them as favorites or delete them.

</details>
