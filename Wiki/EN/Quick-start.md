# Quick start

*Where to begin: how to find your way around, what to do in the first hours, the daily routine and the most common mistakes.*

---

## Where to begin

You have an almost empty village and a thousand tabs to explore: it's normal to feel a little lost. This page is the short road. It doesn't explain everything, and it doesn't try to: it tells you *in what order* to move in the first hours and points you to the right page when you want to understand more.

## The first hours

At the beginning the village produces little, you have no army and there is a whole world to discover. This is a sensible order to move in.

1. **Grow your production.** Build more production structures and enough <img src="../../assets/mini/Abitazioni_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Houses** to have <img src="../../assets/mini/Popolazione_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Population**, because without it you can neither build nor train. Always keep something in construction: time passes even when you're not watching. See [Construction and buildings](Construction-and-buildings.md) and [Resources](Resources.md).
2. **Start a Research.** You carry out one at a time, so it's best not to leave the queue empty. See [Research and Technology](Research-and-Technology.md).
3. **Train your first soldiers and deploy them.** You need Barracks, equipment and population; once ready they must be placed in the structures of the **City**, because that is where they stop whoever attacks you. See [Army](Army.md) and [The City and defense](The-City-and-defense.md).
4. **Try the Barbarian Villages.** They are the safest training ground: press **Explore** to find out what they defend, then attack. See [PVE: Barbarians](PVE-Barbarians.md) and, to understand how the target is built, [Barbarian Cities and Villages](Barbarian-Cities-and-Villages.md).
5. **Read the Report.** It tells you where the attack stalled and what to improve. See [Battle reports](Battle-reports.md).
6. **Check the Quests and your growth.** Every level you gain gives you a point to spend in **Technology**; Quests reward what you are already doing. See [Experience and levels](Experience-and-levels.md) and [Monthly Quests](Monthly-Quests.md).

PVP, meaning challenging other players, only opens at a certain level, shown in the **PVP** panel: there's no rush to get there.

## The daily routine

The game doesn't ask for hours on end: it asks for a regular glance. Here is a check-up round that takes a few minutes.

| When | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| On login | The **Overview**, with the news; the new **Reports**, which show what happened while you were away; the reward of the day in the **GamePass** tab, if you have Gold ([GamePass and daily rewards](GamePass-and-daily-rewards.md)). |
| While playing | The construction, research and training queues: if one is empty, it's wasted time. Resources close to their limit: when a stock is full, that production stops. |
| When you have time | An attack on the Barbarians, the **Quests** to advance and the rewards to claim in the Monthly Quests bar. |
| Before logging out | Something long in the queue, troops deployed in the City and, if you need it, a **Peace Shield**. |

## The most common mistakes

- **Spending Purple Diamonds without thinking** — They are the rarest currency. Before buying something, ask yourself whether it's really the most useful thing right now; for the alternatives see [Shop and Diamonds](Shop-and-Diamonds.md).
- **Leaving the queues empty** — Construction, research and training keep working when you're offline. A full queue before logging out is worth an extra hour of play.
- **Attacking without exploring** — Spies cost little compared to a lost battle. Before striking, look at what awaits you ([Espionage](Espionage.md)).
- **Running out of Arrows** — Archers and Catapults without Arrows fight poorly. Check your stock before an important battle.
- **Spending Technology points in a hurry** — An assigned point can't be taken back, unless you use a dedicated item. Plan where to put them ([Research and Technology](Research-and-Technology.md)).
- **Forgetting the first of the month** — Unclaimed Quests and rewards start over from zero, and the barbarian world is renewed. Claim first, then restart.
