# PVE: Barbarians

*Barbarian Villages and Cities: how to attack, what you earn and how to climb the ladder of levels.*

---

## The world's training ground

Before challenging other rulers, the safest way to test your army is to face the **Barbarians**. They are enemies with no village to defend and no account behind them: they keep loot and experience points, and every victory tells you how much your army is really worth. They are the most regular source of Food, Wood, Stone, Iron and Gold besides what you produce, and one of the ways to earn Diamonds by playing.

Everything is done from the **PVP/PVE** tab, by pressing **PVE** at the top. The panel is called **Barbarians** and the troops to send are chosen, as always, in the **Army to Send** panel (the same for PVE, PVP and Rallies).

## Two types of enemy

| Village | City |
| --- | --- |
| **Personal** camps: they are yours alone, nobody can take the loot away before you and the other players have their own. They are the everyday training ground. | Fortresses **shared** by your whole Kingdom: the loot is richer, including <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteViola_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Purple Diamonds**, but the Cities belong to everyone and run out. The [Kingdoms](Kingdoms.md) page talks about it. |

You switch from one to the other with the **Village / City** selector at the top of the panel. Both Villages and Cities are a **ladder of levels**: the first is always open and **the next one appears only after defeating the previous one**. In the dropdown under the selector you see the levels already reachable, and the beaten ones are marked «✓ defeated».

## How to attack

1. In **Army to Send** choose the levels (I–V) and the quantities of troops. The quantities stay set until you use them.
2. In the **Barbarians** panel choose the type (**Village** or **City**) and the level of the target from the dropdown.
3. If you want to know what awaits you, press **Explore**: your spies cost <img src="../../assets/mini/Oro_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Gold** and deliver you a Report (the [Espionage](Espionage.md) page talks about it).
4. Press **Attack**. If you haven't chosen any troops, the game reminds you and nothing starts.

The two buttons have a short pause after each press, to avoid accidental double submissions. The outcome is immediate: your army comes home and you find everything in the Report.

## How the battle goes

An attack on the Barbarians is a single clash, without the defensive layers of PVP. First the **ranged units** face each other (<img src="../../assets/mini/Arciere_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Archers** and <img src="../../assets/mini/Catapulta_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Catapults** consume <img src="../../assets/mini/Frecce_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Arrows**), then it moves on to the melee. You win when the barbarian army is wiped out. A few important rules:

- **Losses are real.** Fallen soldiers don't come back: they are the ones you sent and who didn't survive. A crushing victory costs little, a narrow victory costs dearly.
- **The Barbarians don't regenerate at every attack.** If you can't eliminate them all, the survivors stay as they were at the end of the clash: you can come back later with other troops and finish the job, without starting from zero. Only the structure's Health and Defense slowly go back up over time.
- **Defeating a target opens the next one.** It's the condition to climb the ladder of levels.
- **An already defeated target can still give you something.** As long as loot remains in its storage, attacking it again may be worth it; when it's empty, there's nothing left to take.

## Loot and experience

If you win, your survivors load on their shoulders what they can carry: their **carrying capacity** counts, which is why the *Transport* research and the roomier classes make the difference. The load is split fairly among the different available resources, and what you can't carry stays in the target's storage.

<img src="../../assets/mini/Exp_1.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Experience**, instead, doesn't depend on carrying capacity: you earn it for the enemies eliminated, even when the battle doesn't go well, and you'll get a conquest bonus when you win. If you want to understand how experience raises your <img src="../../assets/mini/Livello_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Level**, there is a dedicated page later in the wiki.

> [!NOTE]
> **Diamonds from the Cities**
>
> The <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteViola_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Purple Diamonds** of the Barbarian Cities have a weekly limit per Account, shared by all your villages: when you select **City** the panel shows you how many you can still obtain. If the limit is reached, you still receive the other resources, and the cap renews every Monday and with the monthly regeneration of the Cities.

## When everything regenerates

Barbarian Villages and Cities aren't eternal: at the start of each month they are **regenerated**. Your Villages restart from level 1, calibrated on your level at that time; the Cities are back on their feet for the whole Kingdom. Starting over is an opportunity, not a loss: old loots close and new ones arrive.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> **To start off on the right foot**
>
> **Explore before attacking** levels you don't know: it costs a bit of Gold, but it avoids sending the army to lose. **Don't send everything.** You can split the effort over several attacks and keep part of the troops in reserve. **Check your Arrows** if you want to use Archers and Catapults: without ammunition they are worth much less. **Go back to the Villages** regularly: they are the safest source of resources when production isn't enough.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>Why don't I see the next level in the dropdown?</b></summary>

Because you haven't defeated the previous one yet. Eliminate the whole garrison of the current target and the next one will appear.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I lost: do the barbarians recover their strength?</b></summary>

No, they stay with the surviving troops (only the structure's Health and Defense slowly go back up over time). Come back with a stronger army and you'll finish the job.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can other players see or attack my Barbarian Villages?</b></summary>

No, Villages are personal. Cities, instead, are shared by the Kingdom.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I attack whenever I want?</b></summary>

Yes: you decide when and whom to attack, as long as you have free troops to send. The only limit you'll meet concerns the Purple Diamonds of the Cities.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does Attack stay gray?</b></summary>

Almost always because you haven't selected any troops in the Army to Send panel, or because you just pressed the button and it's still resting for a few seconds.

</details>
