# Fiefs and income

*How Fiefs are bought, how Tributes work and how they are exchanged or withdrawn.*

---

## Your income, even when you're away

**Fiefs** are lands you buy with <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteViola_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Purple Diamonds** and that work for you: each one pays <img src="../../assets/mini/Tributi_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Tributes** over time, a special resource that doesn't come from battles and can't be plundered. Tributes accumulate on their own, second after second, even while you're out of the game; whenever you want, you exchange them into Purple Diamonds.

This page is intended for the website. Everything that follows lives in the **Overview** tab, in the **Fiefs** panel.

> [!WARNING]
> **An income that is not guaranteed**
>
> Warriors & Wealth commits to keeping the generation of Tributes active for as long as possible, and the desire is to keep it on always. It is not, however, a promise: we reserve the right to change or remove the generation of Tributes, should it ever be needed. For this reason Fiefs should be experienced as a part of the game, not as a commitment to depend on.

## The Fiefs panel

At the top you find the list of the Fiefs you own, divided by rarity. Below are the actions, in separate groups:

- **Buy** — Spends Purple Diamonds on a new Fief. The cost is written on the button. A few moments pass between one purchase and the next, just to avoid double clicks.
- **Currency exchange** — **Exchange** turns Purple Diamonds into Blue Diamonds, **Exchange Tributes** turns Tributes into Purple Diamonds. In both cases the rate is written in the form.
- **Free reward** — When available, a watched ad gives Purple Diamonds. The prize, the wait between two ads and the daily limit are decided by the server, and the button lights up only when you can use it.
- **Withdrawal** — Visible only when withdrawals are enabled: it converts Tributes into USDT. It is covered at the bottom of the page.

## A surprise purchase

When you buy a Fief you don't choose the rarity: the game draws it, at random, among five levels. The higher the rarity, the more Tributes that Fief produces, but the less likely it is to find it.

| Rarity | How to read it |
| --- | --- |
| **Common Fief** | The most frequent rarity, and the most modest. |
| **Uncommon Fief** | One step up, still easy to find. |
| **Rare Fief** | You start to notice the difference in income. |
| **Epic Fief** | Rare to find and very generous. |
| **Legendary Fief** | The rarest, and the richest. One is also given as the final reward of the <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Silver_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **GamePass Silver** in the [Monthly Quests](Monthly-Quests.md). |

The exact numbers, that is, how many Tributes per second each rarity produces and with what probability you find it, are in the **ⓘ** button next to the panel title. It reads them directly from the server, so they are always the real ones.

> [!NOTE]
> **The Fief can't be chosen**
>
> Once obtained, the Fief stays yours and keeps working. Before buying, check that you have enough Purple Diamonds: if not, the game tells you and spends nothing.

## Tributes

<img src="../../assets/mini/Tributi_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Tributes** are the income of your Fiefs: the icon is in the resource bar, and tapping it the game explains how many Purple Diamonds a single Tribute equals. Three things happen:

- **They accumulate on their own.** The more Fiefs you have, and the rarer they are, the more abundant the flow. There is no stock limit as with other resources, and they keep arriving even while you're offline.
- **They aren't lost in battle.** Unlike civilian resources, Tributes can't be plundered.
- **They are exchanged or withdrawn.** With **Exchange Tributes** you turn them into Purple Diamonds to spend in the game, or, where withdrawals are enabled, you convert them into USDT.

The cycle, then, is this: Purple Diamonds buy Fiefs, Fiefs produce Tributes, Tributes become Purple Diamonds again (or USDT). A circle that rewards patience more than haste.

## Exchanging Tributes

1. Press **Exchange Tributes** in the Fiefs panel: the form opens, with the exchange rate highlighted.
2. Choose how many Tributes to exchange with the **−** and **+** buttons. The preview shows how many Purple Diamonds you receive.
3. Press **Exchange**: the Diamonds arrive immediately. To get Blue Diamonds, then exchange the Purple ones with **Exchange** in the «Currency exchange» group.

## Withdrawing in USDT

If withdrawals are enabled, in the **Withdrawal** group the **Withdraw** button appears: it converts your Tributes into **USDT** (at the rate written in the form) and sends them to an address of yours, on the **Polygon** network. There is no deposit to make first: you start only from what your Fiefs have produced.

1. Open the form with **Withdraw**. Above you find the available Tributes and the **minimum withdrawal**.
2. Write your **USDT address on the Polygon network**. Check it carefully: the game can't recover a transfer sent to a wrong address.
3. Choose the amount with **−** and **+**: each tap is worth the minimum, so the amount is always valid.
4. If you've enabled **2FA** in Options, you receive a code by email to enter in the field that appears; then confirm.

Tributes are deducted immediately, to prevent the same ones from being spent twice. From that moment you can follow the withdrawal in the status window, which goes through these stages:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent** | The transaction has gone out and is waiting to be included in a block. |
| **Confirming** | The network has recorded it and is accumulating the security confirmations. |
| **Confirmed** | Withdrawal completed: the funds have arrived. |
| **Under review** | The team is checking the request. You don't need to do anything: we'll update you as soon as it's resolved. |
| **Failed** | The transfer didn't succeed and your Tributes come back to you. |

The **My withdrawal requests** button opens the history, with amount, status, date and transaction identifier of each request.

### The rules, in brief

- **There is a minimum** for each withdrawal, and a **daily limit** per account.
- **A short pause is needed** between two requests, and while a request is «under review» no others can be made.
- **After disabling 2FA** withdrawals stay suspended for a while: it serves to protect you if someone takes over your session.
- **Withdrawals may be suspended** temporarily, for example during server maintenance; in that case the button flags it.

> [!WARNING]
> **A game, not an investment**
>
> Tributes are a game resource: when and how much you can withdraw depends on the rules in force at that moment, on the limits and on the security checks. Warriors & Wealth does not guarantee earnings of any kind: play to have fun and don't count on withdrawals as an income.

> [!NOTE]
> **A feature we care about**
>
> Withdrawals from the website are an important feature, and we would like never to have to block them. Warriors & Wealth does however reserve the right to suspend, limit or remove them, even for long periods, if technical, security or legal reasons required it. If that happened, the **Withdraw** button flags it.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> **Growing the income**
>
> **Think long term.** A Fief starts earning from the moment you buy it and never stops: the sooner you get it, the more Tributes you accumulate. **Check the ⓘ.** Knowing probabilities and yields helps you decide when to buy. **Decide what to do with the Tributes.** Converting them right away into Purple Diamonds lets you reinvest in new Fiefs; letting them accumulate gives you more choice.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>Can I choose the rarity of the Fief?</b></summary>

No, it's drawn at random with every purchase. The probabilities are in the **ⓘ** box.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Are the generation of Tributes and withdrawals guaranteed forever?</b></summary>

No. Warriors & Wealth tries to keep both active, and would like to always do so, but reserves the right to change or remove the generation of Tributes and to suspend, limit or remove withdrawals. For this reason they should be considered a part of the game and not an income to count on.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do Tributes stop if I don't play?</b></summary>

No, they keep arriving even while you're offline.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can another player steal my Tributes?</b></summary>

No. Tributes can't be plundered.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do I see how many Tributes I receive per second?</b></summary>

For each rarity in the **ⓘ** box of the Fiefs panel.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why don't I see the Withdraw button?</b></summary>

Because at that moment withdrawals are not enabled. When they are, the button and the history appear in the Withdrawal group.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The withdrawal is «Failed»: did I lose the Tributes?</b></summary>

No. When the transfer doesn't succeed, the Tributes are returned.

</details>
