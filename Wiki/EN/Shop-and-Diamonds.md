# Shop and Diamonds

*Packs, items, currencies and payments: what you can buy and how.*

---

## The place where Diamonds are spent

The **Shop** is the tab where **Diamonds** become something concrete: extra time, work queues, protection, bonuses. At the top there are two views: **Packs**, the shop proper, and **Items**, where you buy the items that will end up in the [Inventory](Inventory-and-items.md). The rest of the page takes them one at a time.

Diamonds aren't only bought: you earn them by playing. **Blue Diamonds** come from Quests, from exchanging Purple and from Barbarian loot; <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteViola_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Purple Diamonds** from Quests, from [GamePass](GamePass-and-daily-rewards.md) rewards and from Barbarian Cities (see [Barbarian Cities and Villages](Barbarian-Cities-and-Villages.md)). To exchange Purple into Blue there is the **Exchange** button in the Fiefs tab of the Overview. In the same panel, when available, the **Free reward** box gives Purple Diamonds in exchange for watching an ad. On the website, the <img src="../../assets/mini/Tributi_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Tributes** from Fiefs can be turned into Purple: [Fiefs and income](Fiefs-and-income.md) talks about it.

## The Packs view

Packs are divided into categories, each with its cards. A card shows the icon, the name, what you get, the price and the purchase button. Where there is the **ⓘ** button, press it: the game explains exactly what the pack does and with what limits. After a purchase you see a confirmation message and your balance updates right away.

- <b><img src="../../assets/mini/Vip_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> VIP</b> — A period of bonuses to army strength, to construction, training, research and repair speed, to resource production and to carrying capacity. There are two cards, with the same effect: one is paid in <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteViola_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Purple Diamonds**, the other with real money. Time accumulates up to a maximum. Under the cards you always read whether it's active and how much time is left.
- **Starter Pack** — Two entry packs, I and II, that combine **Purple Diamonds** and <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteBlu_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Blue Diamonds** at a low price. They are a *One-time offer*: they can be bought **only once per account**, and after purchase the card turns off.
- <b><img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteViola_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> Purple Diamonds</b> — Five packs of increasing size: the bigger the pack, the more Diamonds you get per unit of price. You can buy them again whenever you want.
- **Builders, Recruiters, Trap makers** — They open an *extra queue* for a period: an extra site for **Construction**, an extra slot for **Training** and one for **Traps**. They cost **Blue Diamonds**, come in two duration sizes and the time accumulates up to a maximum. Once the cap is reached, the card turns off.
- **Peace Shield** — It protects the village from player attacks. The purchased time adds to the one already active, in three sizes, up to a maximum. Remember that while the shield is active you can't attack other players (see [PVP: challenging players](PVP-challenging-players.md)). If a [Rally](Rallies.md) is open against you, the purchase is blocked.
- **GamePass** — The two timed passes, <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Silver_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **GamePass Silver** and <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Gold_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **GamePass Gold**, explained in [GamePass and daily rewards](GamePass-and-daily-rewards.md). They are paid with real money and accumulate up to a cap.

> [!NOTE]
> **When a card is off**
>
> A card that is off is not an error, and a label on the card tells you why: *Limit reached* when you already have the maximum accumulable time, *Already purchased* for the Starter Packs, *Not enough Diamonds* when your balance isn't enough. Purchases in Diamonds are instantaneous.

## The Items view

Here you buy the items you'll then use from the Inventory: resource packs, boosts, troops, speed-ups and so on. The categories are the same as in the Inventory, and each card shows the same sizes, each with its price and the required currency, **Blue** or **Purple** depending on the item.

1. Choose the **category** with the buttons at the top.
2. Tap the **size** you're interested in: you see the price and how many you already own.
3. Set the **quantity** with **−**, **+** or **Max** (the maximum your balance allows), or type it directly.
4. Press **Buy**. The purchase is «all or nothing»: either it goes through in full or you spend nothing.

What you buy goes straight into the Inventory, ready to use whenever you want. Buying activates nothing: timed bonuses start only when you press **Use**.

## Paying with real money on the website

Packs paid with real money on the website are paid in **USDT**, through the blockchain. There's no need to register cards: the game prepares a payment request and you complete it from your wallet.

1. **Choose the pack** and press the purchase button. If you haven't done so yet, the game asks you to confirm that you are **of legal age**: real-money payments are reserved for adults.
2. **A window with the QR code opens.** Scan it with your wallet: the amount and the recipient are already filled in. If your wallet doesn't recognize it, there is a button to switch to the «address only» version.
3. **Pay and wait.** The payment is detected, then a few confirmations on the network are needed: the status in the window updates on its own.
4. **The reward arrives automatically** as soon as the payment is confirmed, with nothing else to do.

The request has an expiry: if you don't pay in time the window tells you and you can simply start over. If you close the QR by mistake, no problem: the **My payment requests** button, above the cards, opens the history with the status of each one and, for those still pending, **Reopen QR**.

> [!TIP]
> **Pay carefully**
>
> Always pay the amount and the address shown by the game, without changes, and from the network indicated in the window. If the pack is no longer purchasable at the moment the payment arrives (for example because you reached the maximum accumulable time), the amount is sent back to the address you paid from.

## On the Android app

In the Android app paid purchases go through **Google Play**, in compliance with its rules: the cards are the same for Starter Pack, GamePass, VIP and Purple Diamonds, but the price is the one shown by Google and the reward arrives when Google confirms the purchase. The USDT payment cards don't appear. Purchases in Diamonds (Builders, Peace Shield and the rest) work exactly as on the website. If at some moment Google Play purchases aren't active, the cards stay visible but off.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> **Spending well**
>
> **Read the ⓘ before buying.** Duration, limits and exact values are there, and always up to date. **Buy time when you need it.** Builders and Recruiters are worth a lot if you have plenty of things to launch together, little if you're waiting. **Don't forget the Shield.** An active shield protects you while you're away, but prevents you from attacking. **Keep Diamonds moving.** Often a Purple exchanged into Blue or an item bought at the right moment is worth more than a pack left sitting there.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>I can't buy a pack, why?</b></summary>

There are three typical reasons: not enough Diamonds, you already have the maximum accumulable time, or it's a Starter Pack already purchased. A label on the card says it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do I find the items I bought?</b></summary>

In the **Inventory** tab, in the item's category. If you don't see them, tick «Also show items you do not own» to check that they exist and how many you have.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I exchange Blue Diamonds into Purple?</b></summary>

No, the exchange goes in one direction only: from Purple to Blue.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does the pack I bought apply to all my villages?</b></summary>

No. Purchases go to the village you are logged in with at the moment you pay.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I paid but the reward didn't arrive.</b></summary>

Wait for the network to confirm the payment: the request status, in the history, tells you where it stands. If something doesn't add up, write to support naming the request.

</details>
