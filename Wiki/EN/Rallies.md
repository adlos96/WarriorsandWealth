# Rallies

*Joining the Kingdom's troops in a single assault: how to create one, how to join, what happens to whoever is in the crosshairs and how the loot is split.*

---

## Why Rallies exist

Not every enemy goes down alone. Some Barbarian Cities keep garrisons too large for a single army, and some players have fortified their village to the point of making a solitary siege useless. **Rallies** exist for this: they let several rulers of the Kingdom join their troops in a single coordinated attack.

A Rally doesn't merge the armies into an anonymous host. Everyone fights **with their own statistics, their own research and their own bonuses**: the forces on the field are added up, not the differences between those who bring them.

## Where to find them

In the **PVP/PVE** tab, by pressing **Rallies** at the top. The troops to send are chosen only once in the **Army to Send** panel, the same one you use for single attacks: it applies both to create a Rally and to join one already open. Below you find two lists, **Open rallies** (those of your Kingdom you can take part in) and **My participations**.

## Creating a Rally

1. In **Army to Send** choose the troops you want to open the Rally with. You need **at least one**: there are no empty Rallies, not even for whoever founds them.
2. Choose the **target**: a **Barbarian City** of your Kingdom (the ones already defeated are marked) or a **Player** of the Kingdom.
3. Choose the **Duration**: for how long the Rally stays open to others (5, 10, 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes).
4. If you want, tick **Alliance only** (see below).
5. Press **Create rally**. The chosen troops are committed immediately.

> [!NOTE]
> **Against a player**
>
> A Rally against another player follows the same conditions as a single PVP attack: PVP must be unlocked (you see the required level in the PVP screen) and neither you nor the target may have the **Peace Shield** active. The target must be in your Kingdom.
>
> The target is not caught by surprise: as soon as you create the Rally they receive a warning (see the section «If the Rally is against you» below) and before you can press **Start** you have to wait a short notice period. A Rally against a player is therefore not a stab in the back, but an announced challenge.

## Joining a Rally

In the **Open rallies** list each row shows target, participants and time left. With troops selected in **Army to Send** press **Join**: the troops are committed immediately and the button becomes «Already in». You can contribute troops of **all five levels** and of all four classes. You can only join the Rallies of your own Kingdom, and never the one that has yourself as target: in your row, in place of **Join** you find the text «You are the target».

If you want to change your contribution, leave the Rally and re-enter with the right troops.

## Who can do what

| Action | Who | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Join | Anyone in the Kingdom | Your troops are committed to the Rally. |
| Leave | Every participant, until the attack has started | Your troops are immediately available again. |
| Close rally | Only whoever created it | The Rally is cancelled and the troops of all participants go back home. |
| Start | Only whoever created it | The attack begins: from that moment nobody can enter, leave or cancel. Against a player, it only activates after the short notice period. |

A Rally with no participants left, for example because the last one has left, cancels itself.

## Time: a detail that matters

> [!WARNING]
> **Careful**
>
> The Rally **does not start on its own**. When the time runs out without the creator having pressed **Start**, the Rally is cancelled and every participant gets their troops back. Whoever organizes it must therefore remember to start it before the end, when the group is ready.

The chosen duration is therefore a gathering window: short times when your mates are online, long times when you need to give the others a chance to get organized.

If the target is a player, there is also a minimum waiting time before you can press **Start**. If you try earlier, the game tells you it's too early and the Rally stays as it is. So choose a duration long enough to leave room for that wait.

## The battle

When the creator presses **Start**, the clash is resolved immediately, with the same basic rules as the other battles: first the ranged phase, then the melee, layer after layer. Some things are typical of Rallies:

- **Everyone fights with their own statistics.** Defense and health of each group depend on its research and bonuses: whoever is better prepared loses less, whoever is less prepared loses more.
- **Losses are split according to the contribution.** Whoever brings more troops onto the field absorbs more of them.
- **Everyone receives their own Report**, with their losses, their loot and their experience, and the survivors return to their own village.

## Loot and experience

The loot obtained, whether it's a Barbarian City or a player, is split among the participants **in proportion to how much each one manages to carry with their surviving troops**. In other words, bringing more troops, and better equipped for carrying, means a bigger slice. Experience instead follows the contribution in battle.

The rules on limits stay personal. The **Purple Diamonds from the Cities** count toward each participant's weekly limit and, against players, the same Diamond limits as single PVP apply. Whoever has reached their limit keeps receiving the other resources.

A Rally against a Barbarian City always hits the shared Cities of the Kingdom, never the personal Barbarian Villages: we talk about it on the [Kingdoms](Kingdoms.md) page too.

## If the Rally is against you

When someone opens a Rally that has you as target, the game doesn't leave you in the dark. You have three signals, designed to give you time to prepare:

| A message | A red row | An alarm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In the **History** a notice appears when the Rally is opened, another when the attack starts and yet another if it ends without having started. | In **Open rallies** the Rally is highlighted in red with the **Against you** label. You always see it, even if its creator ticked **Alliance only**. | Above the list a notice appears with the **Go to the City** button, and a red dot flags the danger on the **PVP/PVE** tab and on the **Rallies** button. |

> [!WARNING]
> **The Peace Shield won't save you at the last minute**
>
> As long as there is a Rally open or in progress against you, you can't buy the **Peace Shield** in the Shop (the card reminds you that there is a Rally open against you) nor activate it from the **Inventory**. The shield must therefore be kept ready beforehand, not looked for when the Rally is already open.

What to do in the meantime is up to you: reinforce the garrisons, repair the damaged structures and deploy the traps ([The City and defense](The-City-and-defense.md)), or empty the richest storages before the attack arrives. The Rally may also never start: if the creator doesn't press **Start** in time, it expires on its own.

## The «Alliance only» Rallies

When you create a Rally you can tick **Alliance only**. Until a real alliance system exists in the game, this option has a very concrete effect: the Rally is visible and usable only by you, with a single exception: if the target is a player, they see it anyway, because they must be able to know what is being prepared against them. It's a preview of a broader feature, designed for the moment when organized groups want to move without opening up to the whole Kingdom.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> **To do better**
>
> **Explore before creating.** Understanding what's ahead tells you how many troops are really needed and how long to wait. **Choose the duration based on who you can involve:** 5 minutes if your mates are already ready, more if you need to leave time for the others. **Remember to start.** A Rally full of troops that expires is a missed opportunity for everyone. **If you are the target,** use the notice period to get the City in order instead of waiting and hoping: every extra defensive layer is enemy troops falling before they reach the Village.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>Can I join the Rally of another Kingdom?</b></summary>

No. A Rally belongs to the Kingdom of whoever creates it and can be seen and used only by the players of that Kingdom.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where are my troops while the Rally is open?</b></summary>

They are committed to the Rally and no longer part of your available army, until they come back: through a *Leave*, through a closure, because the Rally expired or because the battle is over, in which case the survivors return.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What happens if the targeted player changes Kingdom?</b></summary>

The Rally closes on its own and the troops of all the participants go back home.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I create a Rally without an army?</b></summary>

No: you need at least one troop to create it and to take part in it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>They opened a Rally against me: can I activate the Peace Shield?</b></summary>

No, as long as the Rally is open or in progress the Peace Shield can't be bought or activated. You can however prepare your defenses, and if the creator doesn't start in time the Rally expires on its own.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I know there's a Rally against me?</b></summary>

You receive a message in the **History**, the Rally's row is red with the **Against you** label and a red dot appears on **PVP/PVE** and **Rallies**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why can't I press Start right away?</b></summary>

If the target is a player a short notice period is needed after creation, so that whoever is in the crosshairs has time to notice. Try again shortly after.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I take part in a Rally against me?</b></summary>

No, the **Join** button is replaced by the text «You are the target».

</details>
