# Glossary

*The words of the game explained in a few lines, with a link to the page that covers them in depth.*

---

## The words of the game

Warriors & Wealth has a small vocabulary of its own, and many words that seem obvious have a precise meaning: a *Village* can be yours or a barbarian camp, a *Level* can be yours or a unit's. Here they are gathered in a few lines, grouped by topic, with a link to the page that really explains them. If you're looking for a specific word, the search at the top finds it everywhere.

> [!NOTE]
> **Two similar words**
>
> <img src="../../assets/mini/Exp_1.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Experience** and **Quest points** are two separate counts: the first raises your <img src="../../assets/mini/Livello_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Level** and comes only from battles, the second fills the Monthly Quests bar and show «Exp» on the row. In the same way, the player's **Level** and a troop's level (I–V) are not the same thing.

## Village and resources

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/Abitazioni_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Houses** | The civil structure that produces <img src="../../assets/mini/Popolazione_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Population**. See [Construction and buildings](Construction-and-buildings.md). |
| **Barracks** | The structure that houses the troops of a class: without the right Barracks you can't train them, and their maximum number depends on how many you have. See [Army](Army.md). |
| **Queue** | The list of what waits its turn. Constructions, trainings and traps start together only up to the number of Builders, Recruiters and Trap makers you have; the rest waits on its own. |
| **Builders** | How many structures can be built at the same time. You have one by default; more come from the Shop and from certain items. See [Construction and buildings](Construction-and-buildings.md). |
| **Recruiters** | How many units can be trained at the same time. See [Army](Army.md). |
| **Trap makers** | The trap construction queue: one by default, one more with the dedicated Shop pack. |
| **Limit** | The maximum stock of a resource. When you reach it, that production stops until you spend something. The more production structures you have, the higher the limit goes. |
| **Upkeep** | The continuous cost, in Food and Gold, paid by troops and military structures even when they are idle. See [Resources](Resources.md). |
| **Workshop** | The military structure that produces equipment: Swords, Spears, Bows, Shields, Armor and Arrows. In espionage reports you find them as «Workshop». |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/Popolazione_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Population** | Those who work, build and fight. It is needed to build, train and research, it only grows with Houses and can't be plundered. |
| **Net and gross production** | On a resource's card: the *gross* is how much the structures produce, the *net* is how much actually comes in after consumption. The second is the one that counts. |
| **Civilian resources** | Food, Wood, Stone, Iron and Gold, plus Population. They are the only ones that can be plundered. See [Resources](Resources.md). |
| **Military resources** | Swords, Spears, Bows, Shields, Armor and Arrows: the army's equipment. They are produced in the Workshops. |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/Frecce_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Arrows** | The ammunition of Archers and Catapults. Unlike the other military resources they are consumed in battles, and if they run out ranged units are worth much less. |
| **Speed up** | The button that spends <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteBlu_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Blue Diamonds** to remove time from a construction, a training or a research in progress. It shows in advance how much you save. |
| **Demolish** | Instantly removes a structure, but refunds nothing of what was spent. |

## Army and defense

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Troop classes** | Warriors, Spearmen, Archers and Catapults. Each class exists in five levels, from I to V. |
| **Unit level** | The degree of training (I–V): the higher it is, the more Health, Attack, Defense and carrying capacity. Levels after the first are unlocked with Technology. |
| **Carrying capacity** | How much loot a unit can carry away. The more carrying capacity the survivors have, the more you plunder. It grows with the unit's level and with the *Transport* research. |
| **Dismiss** | Removes a certain number of free units in the village. It refunds nothing, but frees space in the Barracks and lightens the upkeep. |
| **City** | The fortified part of the village: six defensive structures in a row, where you deploy troops and traps. It is also the name of the tab. See [The City and defense](The-City-and-defense.md). |
| **Defensive layer** | The number (from 1 to 6) of each defense: Entrance, Walls, Gate, Towers, Center, Castle. Whoever attacks crosses them in this order. |
| **Village** (in battle) | The final point of the defense, after the six layers, where the troops left at home fight. If they fall, the attack succeeded. |
| **Garrison** | The soldiers assigned to a defensive structure. Each structure has a number of places, which Research can increase. Those in garrison still cost Food and Gold. |
| **Move** | The button that takes troops from the Village to a structure and vice versa, from the **Garrison** panel. |
| **Traps** | Caltrops, Hedgehogs, Pavises, Greek Fire and Ballistae. They are built, deployed at the Entrance and Walls, can't be withdrawn and, when they go off against an attack, they are destroyed. |
| **Repair** | Starts the repair of a damaged Health or Defense bar, one step at a time. **Repair All** starts them all at once. |
| **Health and Defense** | The bars of a defensive structure: they absorb blows during the melee along with the soldiers. Entrance and Center don't have them. |

## World and battle

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Kingdom** | The community of villages you play in: you share opponents, Barbarian Cities and Rallies with the other inhabitants. Outside your Kingdom nobody can reach you. See [Kingdoms](Kingdoms.md). |
| **Kingdom change** | The item used to move the village to another Kingdom. It is bought in the Shop, among the Items. |
| **Merge** | When the administrators join two nearly empty Kingdoms, moving everyone to a livelier one. It is free and loses nothing. |
| **Barbarians** | The fixed enemy of the world: they aren't players, and they keep loot and Experience. They exist in two forms, Villages and Cities. See [Barbarian Cities and Villages](Barbarian-Cities-and-Villages.md). |
| **Barbarian Village** | A *personal* camp: everyone has their own, calibrated on their own level. It gives no Purple Diamonds. |
| **Barbarian City** | A fortress *shared* by the whole Kingdom, with richer stocks and, only here, Purple Diamonds. Whoever takes the loot removes it from the others. |
| **Level ladder** | The Barbarians, both Villages and Cities, are a ladder of twenty levels, from the easiest to the hardest: the next one opens only after defeating the previous one. |
| **Monthly renewal** | On the first day of the month the Barbarians start over, and the Quests and their rewards begin again. What you haven't claimed is lost. |
| **PVE** | The clash against the Barbarians. It is in the **PVP/PVE** tab. See [PVE: Barbarians](PVE-Barbarians.md). |
| **PVP** | The clash between players of the same Kingdom. It opens at a certain level, shown in the PVP panel. See [PVP: challenging players](PVP-challenging-players.md). |
| **Army to Send** | The panel where you choose the troops for an attack. It is the same for PVE, PVP and Rallies. |
| **Power** | The overall strength of a village, which adds up structures, army and research. In the PVP list you see it colored relative to yours. It doesn't say how the defenses are deployed. |
| **Rally** | A joint attack: several players of the Kingdom join their troops against a Barbarian City or a player. The creator must press **Start** before the deadline, and whoever is the target is warned. See [Rallies](Rallies.md). |
| **Alliance only** | A Rally option designed for future organized groups. For now it makes the Rally visible only to whoever creates it (and to the target, if it's a player). |
| **Peace Shield** | It protects the village from player attacks, but while it's active you can't attack others. Against the Barbarians you fight normally. |
| **Explore** | The Espionage button: it sends the spies, costs Gold and produces an Espionage Report. See [Espionage](Espionage.md). |
| **Espionage strength and Stage** | How capable your spies are, and how many sections of the report they manage to open. Where they don't reach, «????» appears. |
| **Counter-Espionage** | The research that makes you harder to spy on. It reduces the strength of enemy spies. |
| **Reports** | The report the game writes after a clash or an espionage: Battle Report, Espionage Report, Espionage detected. See [Battle reports](Battle-reports.md). |
| **Plunder and Lost** | The two weekly limits on Diamonds in PVP: the ones you can take and the ones you can lose. They renew every Monday. |

## Growth and rewards

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/Exp_1.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Experience** | Earned only through battles, even when defending, and fills the amount for the next Level. See [Experience and levels](Experience-and-levels.md). |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/Livello_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Level** | The mark of your growth. Each level gives one technology point. It doesn't measure the village's strength. |
| **Research** | Improvements in levels, bought with resources and time, one at a time. See [Research and Technology](Research-and-Technology.md). |
| **Technology** | The tree of points you earn by leveling up. It gives permanent bonuses and unlocks the units and traps of the higher levels. |
| **Unit unlock, trap unlock** | The special Technology cells that open levels II, III, IV and V of troops and traps. |
| **Technology reset** | The item that clears the tree and returns all the points. It is the only way to take back points already assigned. |
| **Inventory** | The tab where you use the items you own and see the active bonuses. See [Inventory and items](Inventory-and-items.md). |
| **Size** | The measure of an item: the same bonus or pack can exist shorter or longer, smaller or bigger. |
| **Monthly Quests** | The goals of the month. Completing them fills the Quest points bar, which unlocks rewards along the way. See [Monthly Quests](Monthly-Quests.md). |
| **Monthly Rewards** | The bar of the same-named panel, with its milestones. Each milestone has a Normal reward and, with Silver, a second one. |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Silver_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **GamePass Silver** | The timed pass that unlocks the second row of rewards of the Monthly Quests. See [GamePass and daily rewards](GamePass-and-daily-rewards.md). |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/GamePass_Gold_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **GamePass Gold** | The timed pass of the **Daily Rewards**: a series of ninety consecutive days, which restarts if you skip a day. |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/Vip_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **VIP** | The period of bonuses to the village's strength and economy. It isn't a GamePass: it gives bonuses, not Diamonds. |

## Currencies and purchases

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteBlu_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Blue Diamonds** | The everyday currency: they speed up work and pay for many packs and items. You earn them by playing. |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/DiamanteViola_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Purple Diamonds** | The rarest currency. They are used for Fiefs and for important things, and are exchanged into Blue Diamonds (not the other way round). |
| **Exchange** | The Fiefs tab button that turns Purple Diamonds into Blue Diamonds, at the rate written in the form. |
| **Shop** | The purchases tab, divided into **Packs** and **Items**. See [Shop and Diamonds](Shop-and-Diamonds.md). |
| **One-time offer** | A pack, like the Starter Packs, that is bought only once per account. |
| **Fief** | A land bought with Purple Diamonds that produces Tributes. The rarity, from Common to Legendary, is drawn at random by the game. See [Fiefs and income](Fiefs-and-income.md). |
| <img src="../../assets/mini/Tributi_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Tributes** | The income of Fiefs: they arrive on their own, even offline, and can't be plundered. They are exchanged into Purple Diamonds with **Exchange Tributes**. Their generation is not guaranteed forever (see the [Frequently asked questions](FAQ.md)). Where withdrawals are enabled, they can also be converted into USDT with **Withdraw**. |
| **Free reward** | The Overview box that, when available, gives Purple Diamonds for a watched ad. |

## Account and menu

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Your game profile, to which one or more villages can be linked. Some limits, like the weekly one on the Cities' Purple Diamonds, apply per Account and not per single village. |
| **Player menu** | It opens from your name, at the top. It gathers what concerns you more than the village: Change Village, profile picture, Wiki, Kingdoms, Support, Options and Privacy policy. |
| **Change Village** | The player menu entry to switch to another of the villages of your Account. |
| **2FA** | Two-factor authentication via email, which can be enabled in **Options**: at login and before every withdrawal a code arrives for you to enter. |
| **Support** | The player menu entry to write to the team: complaints, bug reports and reports of other players. |
