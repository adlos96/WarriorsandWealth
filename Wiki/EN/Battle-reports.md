# Battle reports

*How to read Reports: the list, the favorites and the detail of a clash, phase by phase.*

---

## The diary of your wars

Every clash that concerns you leaves a trace. When you attack, when you're attacked, when you send spies or when someone spies on you, the game writes a **Report** and keeps it for you. It isn't just an archive: it's the tool you use to understand *why* a battle went the way it did and how to make it go better next time.

You find them in the **PVP/PVE** tab, by pressing **Reports** in the row of buttons at the top, next to **PVE** and **PVP**. A list opens: tap a row and the detail opens.

## Three types of Report

| Detail title | When it arrives |
| --- | --- |
| **Battle Report** | After every clash: attacks on Barbarians, PVP, Rallies. Both sides receive it, each with its own point of view. |
| **Espionage Report** | When you press **Explore**. The [Espionage](Espionage.md) page talks about it. |
| **Espionage detected** | When your sentries notice that someone tried to spy on you. This too is covered in the [Espionage](Espionage.md) page. |

## The list

Each row tells the clash at a glance: the type, the opponent, the outcome and the date. The outcome is always written from *your* point of view: if you were attacked and repelled the assault, you read **Victory**, even though the attacker was the other one. On the row you also find:

- **New** or **Read**. Reports never opened are highlighted, so you never miss a report that arrived while you were away. Careful: the game remembers what you've already read on *that device*, so by opening the game on another phone or computer you may see as new some Reports you've already opened.
- The favorites **star**. A starred Report stays marked and you find it again by pressing **★ Favorites** above the list; **All** shows them all again.
- The **✕** to delete. Before deleting the game asks for confirmation, with an extra warning if the Report is among the favorites. Deletion can't be undone.

The most recent Reports are at the top and the list is divided into pages: **Back** and **Next** appear under the list when there are enough to fill more than one.

## Inside the Battle Report

The detail of a battle is long because it tells the whole clash, but it's always ordered the same way. From top to bottom:

- **The two sides** — At the head you find **Attacker** and **Defender**, with the name and the *Strength* of each before and after the clash. The side that is you is marked *You* and whoever won carries the *Winner* mark. Looking at how much strength was lost tells you right away how hard the battle was.
- **Attacker or Defender** — Two buttons let you choose which side to read the report from. It opens from yours, but you can switch at any time: as a defender, for example, you see where your garrisons held; as an attacker, where the losses were heaviest. The resource balance also changes, becoming *Plundered Resources* or *Lost Resources*.
- **The phases of the clash** — In PVP there is one phase for each **defensive layer** crossed, numbered and with the name of the structure (**Entrance, Walls, Gate, Towers, Center, Castle** and finally **Village**): the order is that of the battle. Against Barbarians the clash is just one.
- **The final balance** — At the bottom is the verdict, **Victory** or **Defeat**, together with the total <img src="../../assets/mini/Exp_1.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Experience** you earned. In clashes with Barbarians, before the verdict, you also find the statistics of their troops, divided by level (I–V).

### Inside a phase

Every phase has the same layout. At the top the structure's status: the **Health** and **Defense** bars show the starting value and the final one, with the lost part highlighted in red. Entrance and Center have no bars because they defend only with soldiers. Then, in the order in which they happen:

1. **Traps.** If the structure had any, a table says how many there were, how many went off and how many remained.
2. **Ranged Phase.** It appears when someone fought from afar or consumed <img src="../../assets/mini/Frecce_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Arrows**. A table reports, for each troop class involved, deployed, fallen and survivors.
3. **Melee.** The melee, with the same table: for each troop class how many soldiers entered, how many fell and how many survived. Classes that did not take part do not appear.
4. **Phase experience**, and the **Arrows** used. If there weren't enough for all the <img src="../../assets/mini/Arciere_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Archers** and <img src="../../assets/mini/Catapulta_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Catapults**, the report says so plainly with the «Low on arrows» warning and also indicates how many would have been needed.

## How to read it to learn

A Report read in a hurry only tells you who won. Read calmly, it tells you how to improve. Three questions are almost always worth asking:

> [!TIP]
> **The three questions**
>
> **Where did the attack stop?** If you lost, the last phase of the report is where your army fizzled out: soldiers or statistics were missing there, not elsewhere. **Were the Arrows enough?** A low-arrows warning alone explains many unexpected defeats. **What's left to fix?** As a defender, look at which structures lost Health and Defense and how many traps went off: those are the things to repair and rebuild ([The City and defense](The-City-and-defense.md) talks about it).

## Reports and Rallies

[Rallies](Rallies.md) also produce Reports: each participant receives their own, with their troops, their losses, their loot and their experience. And whoever is attacked by a Rally finds the report of the clash in the same list, as for any other defense.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>Why does a Report I already opened show as «New» again?</b></summary>

Because the game marks Reports as read only on each single device. If you change phone, browser or computer, the ones already read elsewhere may reappear highlighted.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I recover a deleted Report?</b></summary>

No, deletion is permanent. If you're not sure you want to throw it away, put it among the favorites.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does the battle say «Defeat» but I lost few troops?</b></summary>

Because the outcome depends on the final clash, not on losses. Read the phases to understand where your army stopped.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why do some sections not appear in my report?</b></summary>

Because there was nothing to show in the clash: for example, a structure without traps has no traps table, and a battle without ranged troops has no Ranged Phase.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How accurate are the numbers of espionage Reports?</b></summary>

It depends on the strength of your spies: with weak spies many values are estimates. The [Espionage](Espionage.md) page explains how to read them.

</details>
