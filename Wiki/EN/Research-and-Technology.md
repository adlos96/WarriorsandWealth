# Research and Technology

*How to make the village and the army stronger: tiered research and the technology tree.*

---

## Two roads to becoming stronger

Building and training give you more things; **Research** and **Technology** make the ones you already have better. They are two distinct systems, with two tabs in the bottom bar, and they complement each other:

| Research | Technology |
| --- | --- |
| Improvements in **levels** that you buy with resources and time: faster training, stronger troops, sturdier defenses. One research at a time, and time keeps running even if you're offline. | A tree of **points** that you earn by leveling up. They give small permanent bonuses and, above all, **unlock the units and traps of the higher levels**. |

## Research

In the **Research** tab you find three panels: **General**, **Army** and **City**. Each row is a research with its current level: press the research button to start the next level, or the **i** button to read what it does and what it costs. The cost and time of the level you are about to buy are always shown to you.

### General

They concern the whole village.

- **Construction**, **Training**: they reduce the time to build structures and to train troops.
- **Production** and <img src="../../assets/mini/Popolazione_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Population**: they increase, respectively, what the production structures produce and the inflow of citizens.
- **Transport**: it increases the carrying capacity of all units, that is, how much loot they can carry away.
- **Traps**: it increases how many traps each defensive structure can hold (Entrance and Walls).
- **Repair**: it improves the automatic repair of the City defenses.
- **Espionage** and **Counter-Espionage**: they strengthen your spies and, conversely, defend you from other people's.

### Army

For each of the four classes (Warriors, Spearmen, Archers, Catapults) there are four researches: **Attack**, **Health**, **Defense** and <img src="../../assets/mini/Livello_V2.png" width="20" height="20" align="absmiddle" alt=""> **Level**. They work like this: the first three improve the statistics of *all* the units of that class; the **Level** is the «ceiling» that allows you to push them higher. If an Attack, Health or Defense research refuses to start, you usually need to raise the Level of that class first.

### City

For each defensive structure (**Entrance, Gate, Walls, Towers, Center, Castle**) you can research the **Level** and then **Health**, **Defense**, **Garrison** (how many soldiers it can house) and, for Entrance and Walls, **Traps**. Not every structure has all of them: the panel shows only the available ones.

> [!NOTE]
> **The rules of research**
>
> One research at a time: while it's in progress, the others wait. Resources are paid when it starts. Almost every research also has a **minimum player level**, which the game tells you when you lack it, and the ones on unit statistics depend on their Level. To shorten the wait there are the VIP and Technology bonuses.

## Technology

Every time you level up you earn **one technology point**. You spend them in the **Technology** tab, where the tree is divided into **five research branches**. Each one is a grid with three columns of cells: on your screen you can clearly see which are open and which are still locked.

- **The bonus cells.** Every point you invest in a cell gives a small **permanent** bonus: more resources, roomier storages, less upkeep, troops that are stronger or faster to train, sturdier structures, better spies... By selecting a cell you see what it does, how much each point is worth and how many points you can put in it.
- **The unlocks.** In each of the first four branches there are two special cells, one point each: the **Unit unlock** and the **Trap unlock**. They open, in order, levels II, III, IV and V of troops and traps. The fifth branch is made only of bonuses.
- **How cells open.** In a column, a cell opens when the one above it has collected a little more than half of its points. A new branch opens when you have completed one of the three columns of the previous one. If a cell is locked, tapping it makes the game tell you what it is missing.

### Assigning points

1. Open the **Technology** tab and tap a cell: the details appear.
2. Use **+1** (or **Max**) to choose how many points to spend.
3. Press **Confirm**: the game reminds you one last time that points, once assigned, can no longer be taken back.

> [!WARNING]
> **Points are not refunded**
>
> Once assigned, a point **can't be taken back**. The exception is the **Technology reset** item, which clears the whole tree and returns all your points. Before spending them, think about what kind of village you want: a resource-oriented path, a military one or a defensive one.

The bonuses obtained with Technology add up to those of the VIP and you find them summarized in the **Statistics** tab.

## How to think about it

> [!TIP]
> **Don't look for the perfect order**
>
> At the beginning it's best to aim for the **unit unlock** of the next level when you really need it (and not before), and spend the rest where you feel the village is struggling most: an economy that doesn't hold, an army that weighs too much, a defense that gives way. For Research the same principle applies: always start something before closing the game, because time runs even while you're not there.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><b>I clicked Research but it won't start: what's missing?</b></summary>

Three typical causes: you already have a research in progress, you don't have enough resources, or you lack the required player level (or unit Level). The game writes which one.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How many technology points do I have?</b></summary>

One for every level you reach after the first. In the Technology tab you read the available points, the total ones and the ones already assigned.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I unlock level V units right away?</b></summary>

No: the unlocks open in order, one branch after the other, and require having advanced the previous cells.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I spent my points badly: can I fix it?</b></summary>

Only with the **Technology reset** item, which clears the tree and returns all the points to reassign.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does research continue if I leave the game?</b></summary>

Yes: time runs even while you're offline, and research in progress finishes on its own.

</details>
